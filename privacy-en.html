Kintory Privacy Policy

Effective from Kintory’s first app store release.

The operator of Kintory publishes this policy to explain how personal information is handled and how users can manage it. Kintory stores user-entered medical visit, expense and insurance reimbursement records and attached documents on the user’s phone. It does not offer account registration, storage on the operator’s servers or automatic cloud synchronization.

1 Purposes of processing

The app processes information on the user’s phone to create, view and edit records for each profile; organize hospital and pharmacy expenses, reimbursement claims and payments; display statistics; store documents; use device authentication; and share, back up or restore files at the user’s request. The operator does not automatically receive records or attachments entered in the app.

When a user sends an email inquiry, the operator processes information in that inquiry to respond, investigate the reported issue and handle privacy requests.

2 Information processed and how it is provided

Profiles: names or nicknames entered by the user and internal profile identifiers. Date of birth, resident registration number, phone number and email address are not required profile fields.

Visits and expenses: visit date, record title, hospital, department, treatments, notes, hospital payments, covered copayments, noncovered amounts, pharmacy name and expenses, payment method, and creation, modification and deletion timestamps.

Reimbursements: linked visits, insurer or insurance name, claim date and status, actual reimbursement amount and payment date, notes, and creation, modification and deletion timestamps. The app does not retrieve claim results from insurers or submit claims to insurers.

Attachments: the content of photos or PDF files that the user takes or selects, file names, types, sizes, attachment timestamps and linked visit identifiers. Documents may contain names, health details or resident registration numbers. Please obscure unnecessary information before attaching a document.

Settings and security: app lock and unclaimed reminder settings, selected profile, onboarding status, display language, default region and currency, and a device storage encryption key. Expense and reimbursement records retain their currency. Backup passwords are used for encryption or restoration and are not transmitted to or separately retained by the operator.

Inquiries: the sender’s email address, any voluntarily provided name or contact details, the message and files the user sends. Medical documents and backup passwords are not required for support.

App records are provided through manual entry, the camera or file picker, or backup restoration. Inquiry information is provided when the user emails the operator. The app does not provide collection features for account information, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, microphone recordings or advertising and behavioral analytics.

3 Health information and other people’s profiles

Information about diseases, symptoms, treatments and prescriptions may constitute sensitive health information under Article 23 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act. The app processes it on the device for the record management features the user selects. It does not offer public posting or upload to the operator’s servers.

Before managing another person’s profile, explain what will be recorded, retained or shared and obtain the necessary consent or lawful authority. For children under 14, consider the legal representative’s authority and the child’s privacy. Adding a profile does not itself verify the person’s or representative’s consent.

Profiles have separate views but are stored on the same phone. Each backup includes all profiles. Check whose information is included before sending a backup to another person.

4 Retention and storage location

Profiles, visit and reimbursement records and documents are retained in the app’s storage on the user’s phone without a preset automatic expiry. Records remain after the app closes.

Deleting a visit moves it to the trash. Deleted visits, related attachments and deleted reimbursement groups can remain stored for recovery or backup and are included in backups. This version does not offer automatic trash emptying or permanent deletion of individual records.

Retention of exported backups and shared documents depends on the selected storage location, recipient and service settings and policies. Editing app records or deleting the app does not automatically remove copies already saved or sent elsewhere.

Inquiry emails are retained while the inquiry and related requests are being handled and deleted once that purpose has been fulfilled. Where retention is legally required, only the necessary information is retained for the applicable period.

5 Third party disclosure and user initiated sharing

The operator does not automatically collect app records or disclose them to healthcare providers, insurers or advertisers.

When the user shares a backup or document, the operating system’s share menu can transfer the file to the selected app, storage location or recipient. Backups are encrypted with the user’s password. Individual photos or PDFs are shared in a form the recipient can read.

Services selected by the user, such as KakaoTalk, Google Drive or iCloud Drive, handle files, transfers and accounts under their own privacy policies. External sharing is optional. Do not send a backup and its password in the same message.

6 Processing providers and international transfers

The current app has no operator server or outsourced cloud storage for app records. The operator does not provide automatic transfer or storage of app records on foreign servers.

Inquiry emails are sent, received and stored through Kakao Mail for the period necessary to handle the inquiry. The user’s email provider and independently selected file sharing or cloud services also process information under their policies and may store it internationally.

Before introducing operator server backups, external analytics or cloud synchronization, the operator will disclose the information involved, purposes, retention periods, processing providers and international transfers, and establish the required separate sensitive information consent and other legal procedures.

7 Deletion and disposal

Records can be viewed and edited in the app. Deleting a visit moves it to the trash. To remove the entire local dataset, use the operating system’s app data deletion or app deletion feature. On Android use the app storage data deletion option; on iOS choose Delete App rather than Offload App. The scope of removal can depend on the operating system and its backup settings.

The app cleans up temporary files used to view or share photos and PDFs. If cleanup is delayed by an abnormal shutdown, it attempts cleanup on the next launch. It does not delete original photos or files or copies that the user has separately saved or sent.

Device storage keys are managed according to the operating system’s secure storage policies. The operator cannot remotely delete files on the phone, in operating system backups or held by recipients.

Electronic inquiry information is deleted once its processing purpose is fulfilled. Any separate paper copies are disposed of by shredding or comparable means.

8 Rights and how to exercise them

Users can view and edit app records, create backup files and transfer or share them. They can change app lock and reminder settings and revoke permissions in the operating system settings.

The operator does not hold device records and cannot retrieve, restore or delete them on a user’s behalf. Requests to access, correct or delete information provided during inquiries, restrict its processing or withdraw consent can be sent to the contact email below. Legal representatives and authorized agents may also make requests within their authority.

The operator will verify identity or representative authority where necessary and respond subject to applicable legal restrictions. Unnecessary resident registration numbers or complete medical documents will not be requested to exercise these rights.

9 Device permissions

Camera and photo access are optional and used to take or select documents such as receipts and prescriptions. File pickers access photos, PDFs or backup files chosen by the user. Users can still manually record visits and expenses without granting these optional permissions.

Face ID, fingerprint or device passcode authentication is handled by the operating system when the user enables app lock. The app and operator do not receive raw biometric information or device passcodes; the app uses the authentication result.

This version does not provide features that use location, contacts or microphone recording permissions. Permissions can be changed in the phone’s settings.

10 Security measures

The app encrypts its record database and stored attachments and manages the device storage key in the operating system’s secure storage. Optional device authentication and encrypted backup and restoration are available.

The operator cannot recover a forgotten backup password. Users should also manage phone security, operating system backup settings, shared files and copies held by recipients. Encryption does not guarantee prevention of every disclosure in every environment.

11 Automatic collection and automated decisions

The current app does not provide cookies, personalized advertising, user behavior analytics or tracking through advertising identifiers. Stored records are not sent to the operator for advertising, sale, research or user assessment.

Expense totals and statistics are calculations of user-entered amounts. The app does not make automated decisions about diagnosis, treatment or insurance payouts. Account, payment or diagnostic information independently processed by operating systems or app stores is subject to their policies.

13 External privacy assistance

Users may report privacy incidents or seek advice through the Korean Privacy Infringement Report Center at privacy.kisa.or.kr, or seek mediation through the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee at kopico.go.kr. See those websites for current contact details and procedures.

14 Effective date and changes

This policy applies from Kintory’s first app store release. Changes and their effective date will be announced in the app’s privacy policy screen and on the public policy page. Where changes to purposes, disclosure or processing providers require separate consent, the applicable consent procedures will be followed.

Kintory is a personal record management tool. It does not prepare or retain official medical records on behalf of healthcare providers, replace professional diagnosis or treatment decisions, or determine insurance payouts.
